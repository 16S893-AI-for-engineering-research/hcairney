## What I added

I added a custom pi skill at `.pi/skills/presentation-plot/SKILL.md`. The skill gives the agent a consistent set of defaults to follow whenever I ask for a plot intended for a presentation, talk, lecture, or slide deck.

The guidance covers:

- readable typography and figure dimensions;
- colorblind-friendly styling and uncluttered layouts;
- consistent formatting across subplots;
- labels, legends, gridlines, uncertainty, and colorbars;
- Matplotlib script structure and high-resolution PNG output; and
- respecting explicit plotting requirements when they differ from the defaults.
